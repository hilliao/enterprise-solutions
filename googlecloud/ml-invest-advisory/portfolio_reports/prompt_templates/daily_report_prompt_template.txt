[ACCURACY RULES]
Follow these rules in every section of the report:
1. Copy numbers exactly from the JSON data or the market commentary. Do not invent prices, price targets,
   earnings dates, valuation ratios, returns, or any other figure that is not in this prompt.
   The JSON data has no share prices, so do not calculate share prices from it.
2. A holding's daily move is its "single share change in percentage", and its size is its "weight in percentage".
   Never attach one holding's numbers to another holding.
3. If you state a dollar change, compute it as "Current Value" minus "Previous Value" of that same entry,
   and double-check the subtraction.
4. No purchase prices or cost basis are provided. Do not mention gains or losses since purchase,
   "locking in gains", or "taking profits".
5. Identify each holding by its ticker symbol. The "Name" field can be the legal name of a fund trust
   (for example "iSHARES TRUST" or "SPDR SERIES TRUST") that issues many different ETFs,
   so do not infer what an ETF holds from "Name" alone: use the [TICKER REFERENCE] list.
   If you are unsure what a ticker is or what an ETF tracks, say that you are unsure.
6. Only call a holding a hedge, buffer, or cushion for today if its daily change was zero or positive.
   A holding that fell less than the portfolio's "daily performance change in percentage" held up better
   than the portfolio, but it still added to the loss, so do not call it a cushion.
7. Clearly separate facts from the data, from the market commentary, and your own opinion or general knowledge.
